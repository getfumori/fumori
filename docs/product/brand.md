# Fumori Brand Direction

## Name and meaning

**Fumori** is the accepted working product name. It is a coined,
Japanese-inspired name formed from:

- `fumi`: writing, text, or letter
- `mori`: forest

It is not a standard Japanese compound. Its intended meaning is:

> A forest grown from your words.

This phrase is descriptive brand guidance. It has not been approved as
required product copy.

## Product metaphor

- Quick captures and Daily Notes are seeds.
- Markdown notes are durable growth.
- Links and relationships form roots, branches, and paths.
- AI reads and maps the forest without silently rewriting Human Notes.

## Character and visual direction

The brand should feel quiet, literary, organic, private, intelligent, and
long-lived.

The current visual direction uses deep forest green-black on warm ivory, an
editorial serif wordmark, and an abstract mark that combines folded pages,
foliage or knowledge paths, and a subtle letter `F`.

## Logo status

The image under [`docs/brand/`](../brand/) is a generated raster concept. It is
directional evidence only: it is not a canonical, production-ready, or
shippable logo.

A future brand-production task should recreate and refine the accepted
direction as an original SVG, then derive horizontal, symbol-only app icon,
monochrome, and dark-mode variants.

## Name clearance

Initial screening found no adjacent note-taking, knowledge-management, or AI
product using the Fumori name. That screening was preliminary, not formal
trademark or legal clearance. Domains, package names, social handles, and legal
availability must be checked again immediately before registration or public
launch.
